## Screenshots (operator capture)

Not generated by the script. Stores require **the app in use** (not login or splash).

Minimum:

- Apple: ≥ 1 iPhone screenshot in the **6.9"** slot (e.g. 1320×2868). iPad not required (iPhone-only V1).
- Play: ≥ 2 phone screenshots; prefer ≥ 4 at 9:16 and ≥ 1080×1920.

Presentation target: 3–5 views of the core loop (signal feed, observation, action plan). Choose the most representative screens; do not pad with login.

How to capture: any local session with **fictional workplace data**. KONOHA is optional in local/dev only; it is not required. Capture **after** the native Spore icon/splash are on the build you screenshot. Place the PNG/JPEG files in this folder (`iphone-69-*.png`, `play-phone-*.png`) and list them in [`store_listing.md`](../store_listing.md) when they exist.

Do not invent marketing mockups that are not the shipped UI.
